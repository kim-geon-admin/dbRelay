## Known limitations

- The only production connector is Oracle.
- Scheduled/background execution, multi-user sharing, parallel or distributed execution, and large-volume retry orchestration are excluded from the first release.
- Automatic SQL generation, transformation scripting, and schema migration tooling are excluded from the first release.
